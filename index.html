ACADEMIC PROFILE

About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Business Administration and Management at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice, Italy.

My academic interests span several areas of economics, particularly macroeconomics, international finance, economic growth, and inequality. I am interested in understanding how economies function, how financial markets interact with the broader economy, and how economic policies and institutions influence prosperity and the distribution of economic opportunities.

I am currently developing my foundations in economic theory, mathematics, and quantitative methods, while exploring different areas of economics and their applications to contemporary economic and financial challenges.

Looking ahead, I hope to pursue advanced studies in economics and develop a career that combines economic research, public policy analysis, and international finance.

ACADEMIC INTERESTS

Research & Interests

Macroeconomics & Monetary Economics

Macroeconomic fluctuations, inflation, interest rates, and monetary policy, with particular interest in how central banks and monetary shocks influence economic stability, employment, and growth.

International Finance & Macro-Finance

International capital flows, financial markets, exchange rates, and investment dynamics, focusing on the interactions between financial conditions and the real economy.

Economic Growth & Development

Long-term economic growth, productivity, structural transformation, and economic development, exploring the determinants of prosperity and economic disparities across countries.

Public Economics & Fiscal Policy

Public finance, taxation, government expenditure, and fiscal policy, with an interest in economic governance, redistribution, and European economic integration.

Labour Economics & Applied Econometrics

Labour markets, employment, wages, and inequality, with an interest in applying quantitative and econometric methods to economic analysis and public policy evaluation.

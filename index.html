ACADEMIC PROFILE

About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Business Administration and Management at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice, Italy, with a strong interest in economics, financial markets, and public policy.

My academic interests primarily focus on macroeconomics, monetary economics, and international finance. I am particularly interested in understanding how monetary and fiscal policies, financial markets, and economic institutions influence economic stability, long-term growth, and development.

My broader interests include public economics, labour economics, and the application of quantitative methods to economic analysis. I am also interested in European economic integration and the evolving economic relationship between the European Union and the United Kingdom.

I am currently strengthening my foundations in economic theory, mathematics, and quantitative methods, with the ambition of pursuing postgraduate studies in economics and, eventually, contributing to academic research and evidence-based economic policymaking.

ACADEMIC INTERESTS

Research & Interests

Macroeconomics & Monetary Economics

Macroeconomic fluctuations, inflation, interest rates, and monetary policy, with particular interest in how central banks and monetary shocks influence economic stability, employment, and growth.

International Finance & Macro-Finance

International capital flows, financial markets, exchange rates, and investment dynamics, focusing on the interactions between financial conditions and the real economy.

Economic Growth & Development

Long-term economic growth, productivity, structural transformation, and economic development, exploring the determinants of prosperity and economic disparities across countries.

Public Economics & Fiscal Policy

Public finance, taxation, government expenditure, and fiscal policy, with an interest in economic governance, redistribution, and European economic integration.

Labour Economics & Applied Econometrics

Labour markets, employment, wages, and inequality, with an interest in applying quantitative and econometric methods to economic analysis and public policy evaluation.
